Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This policy explains how MR International LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our ecommerce website, admin tools, and related APIs.

1. Who we are

MR International LLC operates retail and wholesale ecommerce services, including customer accounts, product catalogs, orders, payments, and support communications.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Account data — name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed), and role (retail, wholesale, admin).
  • Order & payment data — cart contents, shipping details, payment status, and provider references (card numbers are handled by our payment processor, not stored in full on our servers).
  • Technical data — IP address, device/browser metadata, cookies or tokens needed for authentication and security.
  • Support content — messages you send to our support channels.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure accounts, catalogs, checkout, and order tracking
  • Process payments and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Send transactional mail such as OTP and password-reset messages
  • Respond to support requests and improve service reliability
  • Meet legal, accounting, and compliance obligations

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process personal data to perform our contract with you, to pursue legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, and where required by law. Marketing communications, when offered, are sent with appropriate consent or soft-opt-in rules for your region.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with processors who help us operate the service — for example hosting, email delivery, object storage, and payment providers — under confidentiality and data-processing terms. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the platform.

6. Retention

We keep account and order records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal retention requirements. You may request deletion of account data subject to outstanding orders, disputes, or statutory obligations.

7. Security

We use industry-standard controls including encrypted transport (HTTPS where configured), hashed passwords, role-based access, and rate limiting on sensitive API routes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your credentials.

8. Your choices

  • Update profile details from your account settings where available
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by contacting support
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing emails via the message footer or support

9. Children

Our services are intended for business and adult consumers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction).

10. International transfers

Infrastructure and processors may be located in different countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we publish a revision. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
